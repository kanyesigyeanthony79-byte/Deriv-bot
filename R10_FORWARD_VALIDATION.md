# R10 forward shadow validation

## Purpose and safety boundary

This is a forward-only, observation-only evaluation of the existing R10 M15 setup with two precision-entry variants (M5 and M1). It uses the completed candles already fetched by the scheduled multi-timeframe collector.

- No additional historical-data expansion is requested.
- The first collector run initializes a forward-only M15 cursor. Earlier candles are not backfilled as forward observations.
- The M15 direction is frozen when the completed setup candle is first observed.
- M5/M1 triggers are evaluated only inside the next 15-minute entry window.
- Entry is the open of the candle immediately after the trigger candle.
- Each method uses a 2R target and a one-hour observation horizon.
- Precision stops use the most recent five completed trigger-timeframe candles plus a 0.10 ATR buffer; risk above 2.0 setup M15 ATR is rejected.
- Same-candle stop/target touches are marked ambiguous. Incomplete observation windows remain pending; missing continuous coverage is censored.
- All methods are journaled separately. A second, conservative non-overlapping summary reserves the full 75-minute setup/entry/outcome window between selected setups.
- No Telegram alert, production signal, MT5 queue, demo order, or real-money order is generated by this module.

## Collection and persistence

The dedicated **Research Candle Collector (No Alerts)** runs every 15 minutes using GitHub's native schedule. It calls the existing multi-timeframe collector in `RESEARCH_ONLY_MODE=true`, which disables production journal writes, Telegram delivery, and MT5 reporting. The R10 module runs only in that research-only branch, persists `research_r10_forward_shadow_journal.json`, and adds a compact summary to `research_multi_timeframe_data.json`. The separate research journal is committed by the same scheduled workflow.

The journal records its forward start time, last processed M15 epoch, candle coverage, setup direction/score, each method's trigger/entry/stop/target, and outcome status. It does not rewrite the production `trade_journal.json`, production dashboard, or other strategy journals.

## Reading the results

Statuses are intentionally distinct:

- `target`, `stop`, `time_exit`: resolved outcomes with R return.
- `ambiguous`: OHLC data cannot establish which barrier was hit first.
- `pending`: the one-hour outcome window is not complete yet.
- `no_trigger`: the full entry window was observed without a valid trigger.
- `wide_risk`, `invalid_risk`: the entry was rejected by the fixed stop rules.
- `censored`: required candle coverage was missing; this is not a loss or a no-trigger.

The primary summary includes every eligible M15 setup and therefore may contain overlapping observation windows. The non-overlapping sensitivity summary is reported separately; neither view is a compounded portfolio simulation.

## Promotion gate

Do not promote either precision-entry method from the historical pilot or from a handful of forward observations. Review discovery and validation separately, require a meaningful non-overlapping resolved sample, compare expectancy and drawdown, investigate no-trigger/rejection rates, and include realistic spread, slippage, and execution costs before any separate approval. A positive small-sample expectancy is not sufficient evidence.

This forward journal is a research record, not a trading instruction or a promise of profitability.
