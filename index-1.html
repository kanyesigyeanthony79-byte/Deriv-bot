Rules. Signals: the live engine, unchanged, evaluated on each completed candle using only that candle and earlier ones (the same 199-candle window the live scanner sees), with the same 1-hour cooldown. Entry: close of the signal candle. Exit: close of the candle duration candles later. BUY wins if exit is above entry, SELL wins if exit is below; a tie counts as a loss. Flat stake of 1 unit, no martingale. Win pays the payout % minus cost; a loss costs the stake plus cost. Drawdown is the largest peak-to-trough fall in cumulative result. Real entries come seconds after candle close, and real payouts vary by market and duration, so real results would differ.
Diagnostics


  
Important: BUY and SELL are research alerts, not instructions or guaranteed outcomes. V1 does not set contract duration, stop-loss or take-profit for Deriv contracts and cannot place trades. Synthetic indices may not behave like conventional exchange markets. Test signals without staking money. Nothing here shows this strategy is profitable; the score is a heuristic, not a probability of winning.

  

    Engine v2: a signal needs every gate to pass (EMA 20/50 trend and gap, EMA 50 slope, RSI 14 band, candle direction and body, higher-low/lower-high structure, not extended, no spike candle) plus a minimum heuristic score. Only closed 15-minute candles are used. One signal per candle, then a 1-hour cooldown per market. Thresholds are starting values chosen by judgement, not tuned or backtested. No martingale, no recovery sizing, no automatic execution.

    Data: Deriv public market-data WebSocket (no login). An internet connection and an open, visible browser tab are required; Android may pause the page when the screen is off.
