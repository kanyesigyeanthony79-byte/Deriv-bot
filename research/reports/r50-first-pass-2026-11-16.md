## Replay assumptions

- Indicators use completed candles only.
- Direction: EMA20 above EMA50 for BUY, below for SELL.
- Require the completed signal candle close to be on the trend side of EMA20.
- Pullback variant: BUY candle low touches EMA20 plus 0.25 ATR and closes above EMA20 bullish; SELL is the mirrored condition.
- Strong-candle variant: directional candle body is at least 50% of candle range.
- Strong-RSI variant: BUY RSI 55–72; SELL RSI 28–45.
- Entry: next M15 candle open.
- Stop: configured ATR(14) multiple measured on the signal candle.
- Target: configured R multiple of the stop distance.
- Maximum holding period: 16 M15 candles (four hours).
- Minimum spacing: four signal candles.
- Same-candle stop/target touches are classified as ambiguous and excluded from win-rate denominator. Time exits are reported separately and excluded from resolved win/loss denominator.
- Expectancy is the average of fixed target R for wins and -1R for losses, among resolved trades only. It excludes ambiguous and time-exit trades, costs, spread, slippage and execution effects.

## Interpretation and limits

1. The pullback/body/RSI combination is a promising R_50 candidate in this sample.
2. The holdout contains only 14 resolved trades for each selected candidate. The confidence intervals are wide; one or two different outcomes would materially change the percentages.
3. The 0.75R target produces the highest observed hit rate, but a high hit rate alone is not the objective. Expectancy, risk, drawdown, coverage and robustness must all be considered.
4. The EMA20/50 context baseline is a simple comparison, not a complete reproduction of every production engine quality gate.
5. The same archive was used to explore multiple parameter combinations. The final 40% is a chronological holdout, but repeated candidate inspection and a single capture window still leave selection and regime risk.
6. These are M15-only results. They do not validate M1/M5 entry timing.
7. No transaction costs, spread, slippage, rejected fills or broker execution effects are modeled.

## Decision

**Do not promote R_50 yet.** Keep the existing signal engine unchanged. Next, implement a reproducible, tested R_50-only replay harness; lock the candidate parameters before collecting fresh forward data; then evaluate on additional unseen candles and shadow signals. Require a materially larger sample, positive expectancy after realistic costs, controlled drawdown, and stable results before any production change.

This report records an exploratory research result only. It is not a trading recommendation.
