Counting rules: only a delivery-journal entry with status SENT is included in market progress. A generated strategy observation without a confirmed Telegram delivery is excluded. Signal outcomes are read from the original strategy journal and are not inferred from delivery. Pending, TP1-hit, expired and ambiguous records remain visible as their own statuses; they are not converted into wins or losses. Records are deduplicated by signal ID. The tracker reads the original 15-minute production journal, the R10 paper journal (Telegram-confirmed signals only), the multi-timeframe Telegram journal, three forward-shadow strategy journals and the shared shadow Telegram delivery journal. Each source has its own delivery-confirmation rule; only confirmed SENT records count. Outcomes prefer finalOutcome over the intermediate status field. The 18-market target covers the original 15-market list, both 1-second volatility indices, and Jump 75, which is also present in the shared research collector. A zero count means no confirmed delivered record is present in the connected journals; it does not prove that a market was scanned or that no signal occurred.
